# Open questions

Prepared 2026-09-16. Only questions that affect content accuracy, equipment feasibility or future scheduling are included. Until resolved, every candidate remains unavailable for automatic scheduling.

## Necessary clinical review

- **#5 Straight-knee 2-up/1-down calf raise:** Has unilateral eccentric lowering been specifically reviewed, and what floor-level range and support are permitted? The default must remain flat-floor with no heel drop below floor; that constraint does not itself establish readiness.
- **#6 Bent-knee soleus isometric:** Which seated or supported-standing position, knee angle, heel height, laterality, ankle range and external-load status are appropriate?
- **#1–4 and #7–9:** Which should remain optional movement-support content versus enter any active session, and how can they be added without displacing essential rehabilitation or silently increasing total volume? Standing/cable variants need separate stance-tolerance review.
- **#10–12:** Is forward resisted locomotion or backward treadmill gait clinically appropriate at all? No device solution or elapsed time can answer this.
- **#13–18 and #24:** What separately assessed starting load, range, pickup/entry method and total weekly volume are appropriate for each implement? Machine, cable, lever, dumbbell, barbell and trap-bar loads are not interchangeable.
- **#19 Slant-board squat:** What board angle, squat range, stance and support are appropriate? Do not assume heel elevation reduces Achilles demand for this user.
- **#20 Balance-cushion single-leg stance:** What stable-surface balance capacity and support level should precede the inflatable cushion? The foam pad and inflatable cushion are not equivalent.
- **#21–23:** Which entry variants fit the full-body plan without increasing volume at the expense of rehabilitation, and are there relevant wrist, shoulder or elbow restrictions for the proposed support/grip?

## Necessary equipment and feasibility review

- **#10 Weighted-wagon forward push:** What is the wagon manufacturer/model, load rating, fixed push interface, wheel/brake behavior, surface, slope and clearance? A generic sled demo cannot validate the wagon.
- **#11 Weighted-wagon forward pull/march:** Is there a manufacturer-rated hitch, reviewed tether/belt and quick-release method, and how will roll-in toward the user be prevented?
- **#12 Backward treadmill walk:** What treadmill make/model and manual language applies to reverse walking? Confirm minimum speed, incline behavior, emergency-stop access, handrail geometry and a safe method to start/stop without stepping onto a moving belt.
- **#13–14 Trap bar:** What are empty bar weight, available handle heights, plate/floor clearance and the clear route width/length? Define the turn and controlled set-down method for #14.
- **#15–16 Smith machine:** What are rail direction, effective starting resistance and usable safety settings? For #16, can the bench be positively secured, and are bench height/capacity and a suitable bar pad confirmed?
- **#17 Belt-squat calf raise:** Which manufacturer-compatible handle/attachment is owned and intended, and what flat, non-slip foot surface and hand support keep the lever path clear?
- **#18 Belt-squat isometric:** What hold position, lever stop and bailout/exit method are mechanically feasible? The manufacturer page did not verify this use.
- **#19 Slant board:** What are the exact angle/setting, dimensions, surface and rating?
- **#20 Inflatable cushion:** What inflation guidance, non-slip setup and step-on/off method apply to the Trideer cushion (`dyno-pad`)?
- **#7–9 and #23 Cable exercises:** What pulley ratio, displayed increments, attachment/cuff/handle, anchor height and body-support geometry apply? Preserve the displayed stack setting; do not convert it to free-weight pounds.
- **#22 Chin-up:** What safe step-up/step-down and assistance arrangement exists at the pull-up bar?
- **#24 Farmer carry:** What clear, level route, turn method and pickup/set-down area are available?
