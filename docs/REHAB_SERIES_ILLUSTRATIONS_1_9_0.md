## Monochrome correction — 2026-09-18

All nine images and their thumbnails were replaced with black-and-white illustrations using the built-in image-editing tool. The original colored versions are superseded. Models, exercise poses and titles were visually checked against the originals. Production dimensions remain 640 × 960 and 192 × 288.

Final edit prompt used for each exercise: "Edit image 1 only. Convert the entire exercise illustration to strictly black and white / neutral grayscale, matching the clean monochrome educational illustration aesthetic of image 2. Image 2 is style reference only, do not copy its exercise or layout. Preserve image 1 exact exercise title, model identity, pose, equipment, number of figures, composition, full head and feet, portrait 2:3 aspect ratio. Remove ALL blue, skin color, warm tints and other hues, including equipment and title. White background, crisp black text and grayscale shaded illustration. No new labels, no added objects, no changes to exercise mechanics."

Style reference: the existing seated core march illustration. Upload package: `Achilles_Return-rehab-circuits-1.9.0-monochrome-complete.zip`, which supersedes the earlier 1.9.0 package.

Nine AI-generated educational exercise illustrations were created on 2026-09-17 and visually reviewed. Production images are 640 × 960 PNG; mobile thumbnails are 192 × 288 PNG. Full images are under `public/assets/exercises/strength-library/` and thumbnails under `public/assets/exercises/thumbnails/`. Each filename is the corresponding `rehab-*` exercise ID.
