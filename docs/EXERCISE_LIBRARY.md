# Exercise Library Specification v1.0

## Rule
No exercise may appear in an active workout unless:
1. it uses owned equipment (or bodyweight),
2. its phase/capability prerequisites are satisfied,
3. it has an exercise-specific demonstration link,
4. the demo is short or opens directly to the relevant drill rather than a long general lecture,
5. the source is credible (hospital/health system, university, licensed PT/rehab provider, national governing body, or respected S&C professional).

## Core Achilles / calf exercises

### Loaded seated calf raise
- capability: soleus / plantarflexion strength
- typical prescription: 3–4 × 8–15, RPE 6–9
- tempo: 2 sec up / 1 sec hold / 2–3 sec down
- progression: heavier load, then unilateral
- regression: bodyweight bilateral seated raise
- demo source: Royal United Hospitals Bath / NHS foot & ankle videos
- short demo hub: https://www.ruh.nhs.uk/patients/services/physiotherapy/OP_outpatient_service.asp

### Bilateral bent-knee heel raise

## Upper-body module
These support general strength but do not drive Achilles progression.
- DB bench press
- incline DB press
- cable row
- pull-up / band-assisted pull-up
- DB overhead press
- face pull
- dips if personally appropriate

Each should use a NASM or similarly credible exercise-specific demonstration before activation.

## Conditioning
### Easy bike
- 15–40 min, RPE 3–6 depending phase
- equipment: exercise bike
- demo not required beyond bike setup guidance; if included, use a brief bike-fit/setup clip.

### Incline treadmill walk
- 10–40 min depending phase
- equipment: incline treadmill
- demo: app text cues sufficient; optional short walking-form clip.

### Weighted wagon backward drag
- 4–6 × 15–25 yd, RPE 5–7 initially
- equipment: weighted wagon
- progress with distance, RPE, quality, and symptom response rather than pretending wagon pounds equal sled pounds
- demo: Resilient Performance Physical Therapy
- direct: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YyaNNPy-FNQ

## Soccer skill demos
For the beginner soccer pathway, prefer federation/academy or reputable coaching sources. Required cards:
- basic ball touches
- inside-foot pass
- receiving first touch
- controlled dribble
- instep shooting
- controlled change of direction with ball

Direct short clips should be curated before the soccer pathway is unlocked in production.

## Demo QA rule
Before production release, automated/static validation should ensure:
- `videoUrl` is non-empty for every active exercise
- two distinct exercises do not accidentally share the same general lecture URL unless the clip is intentionally relevant to both
- `videoType` is one of `short`, `exercise_specific_page`, `governing_body_clip`
- `videoVerifiedAt` exists
- `videoSource` exists
- long-form general rehab videos are rejected from `Short Demo` buttons
