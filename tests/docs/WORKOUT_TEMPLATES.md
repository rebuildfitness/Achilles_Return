# Workout Templates and Exposure Ladders v1.0

The templates below are the menu the deterministic engine may use. A daily workout is selected from an approved weekly plan and modified only by readiness/tolerance rules.

## Strength exercise families
### Straight-knee calf family
1. bilateral standing calf raise
2. assisted single-leg calf raise
3. bodyweight single-leg calf raise
4. dumbbell-loaded single-leg calf raise
5. Smith-supported loaded calf raise
6. heavy Smith calf raise / heavy unilateral variation

### Bent-knee/soleus family
1. seated bilateral calf raise
2. loaded seated calf raise
3. unilateral seated calf raise
4. bent-knee standing calf raise
5. heavier unilateral soleus variation

### Lower-body strength
- belt squat
- DB RDL
- step-up
- hip thrust / bridge
- band or cable hamstring curl
- cable hip abduction/adduction as accessory

### Trunk/balance
- Pallof press
- side plank
- single-leg balance
- balance-pad variation only after stable-floor control

### Conditioning
- easy bike
- incline treadmill walk
- weighted wagon backward drag

## Foundation Strength A
- primary straight-knee calf: 4 × 6–10 @ RPE 7–8
- primary soleus: 4 × 8–12 @ RPE 7–8
- belt squat: 3–4 × 6–10 @ RPE 7–8
- DB RDL: 3 × 8–10 @ RPE 7–8
- cable row/pull-up: 3 × 8–12
- Pallof press: 2 × 10/side

## Foundation Strength B
- soleus: 4 × 10–15
- single-leg heel raise progression: 3 × 6–12
- step-up: 3 × 8/side
- hip thrust/bridge: 3 × 8–12
- band/cable hamstring curl: 3 × 10–15
- DB bench press: 3 × 6–10

## Foundation Strength C
- heavy calf pattern: 3–4 sets
- soleus pattern: 3 sets
- belt squat: 3 × 8–12
- weighted wagon backward drag: 4–6 × 15–25 yd
- trunk/balance: 2–3 sets

## Yellow-day replacements
### Yellow 1
- remove one set from moderate/high Achilles exercises
- keep general strength if symptom-neutral
- no progression

### Yellow 2
- remove impact/high-rate work
- use seated/moderate calf variation
- keep non-provocative global strength
- bike 10–25 min optional

### Yellow 3
- no impact
- easy bike / walking if tolerated
- gentle mobility / trunk / upper body only

## Running ladder
All sessions begin with 5–10 min brisk walk and easy movement prep. End with 5 min walk.
- R1: 6 × 1 min jog / 2 min walk
- R2: 8 × 1 / 1
- R3: 6 × 2 / 1
- R4: 5 × 3 / 1
- R5: 4 × 5 / 1
- R6: 20 min continuous easy
- R7: 25 min continuous easy
- R8: 30 min continuous easy
- R9: 20–30 min easy + 4–6 × 20 sec relaxed stride at ~70%

Do not automatically progress if the next-morning response is borderline/not tolerated.

## Jump/landing ladder
### J0 Landing prep
- snap-down: 3 × 5
- low landing hold: 3 × 4

### J1 Bilateral elastic intro
- bilateral pogo: 3 × 15
- jump-to-stick: 3 × 5

### J2 Bilateral progression
- pogo: 3 × 20
- countermovement jump: 3 × 5
- lateral line hop: 2 × 10 each direction

### J3 Intro unilateral
- assisted/low single-leg pogo: 3 × 8–10/side
- forward hop-to-stick: 3 × 4/side

### J4 Unilateral control
- single-leg pogo: 3 × 12–15/side
- lateral hop-to-stick: 3 × 4/side
- repeated bilateral jumps: 3 × 5

### J5 Reactive
- repeated single-leg hops
- diagonal hops
- low reactive drop/landing drill where appropriate

## Sprint ladder
Warm-up fully; long rest between reps.
- S1: 6 × 10 m @ 60–70%
- S2: 6 × 15 m @ 70–80%
- S3: 5 × 20 m @ 80–85%
- S4: 4–6 × 20 m @ 85–90%
- S5: 4 × 20–30 m >90%
- S6: near-max performance sprinting, clinician/performance assessment recommended

## Deceleration/COD ladder
- D1: jog 10 m -> controlled stop, 4–6 reps
- D2: run 10–15 m -> controlled stop, progressively shorter braking zone
- D3: planned 45° cut, 3 × 4/side
- D4: planned 90° cut, 3 × 4/side
- D5: lateral shuffle + crossover + closeout patterns, 4–6 bouts
- D6: reactive visual/cone call cuts, 4–6 bouts
- D7: basketball-specific reactive movement

## Basketball ladder
### B1 Stationary skills
20–30 min; shooting, passing, ball handling.

### B2 Controlled movement
20–30 min; walk/jog shooting, controlled layups, footwork.

### B3 Planned movement
25–40 min; planned cuts, jump stops, submax closeouts, shooting off movement.

### B4 Higher-speed planned work
30–45 min; stronger accel/decel, rebounding, planned cuts, jump shots.

### B5 Reactive non-contact
30–45 min; reactive closeouts, visual cues, controlled defender movement.

### B6 Controlled half-court
Short bouts, limited minutes, full recovery between bouts as needed.

### B7 Controlled full-court
Limited possessions/minutes, programmed rest.

### B8 Initial unrestricted play
20–30 min cap first session; 30–45 min next if tolerated; then expand.

## Soccer ladder
- SC1: ball touches + passing, 10–15 min
- SC2: receiving + walking dribble, 10–20 min
- SC3: jogging dribble + submax shooting
- SC4: planned COD with ball
- SC5: reactive ball movement
- SC6: limited recreational practice/play
